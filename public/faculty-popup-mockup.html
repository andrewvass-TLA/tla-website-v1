Mockup · Faculty popup redesign

Five popup designs for long-form bios

Every design uses the same content for Josh Mettle: started, current role, short bio, background, full bio, specialty and video. Open one, then use the switcher at the bottom of the screen to flip between them. Press Esc to close.

Design 01

Editorial Split

Closest to today's layout, rebuilt for reading. A dark photo column stays put while the bio scrolls on the right.

  • Short bio becomes the opening lede
  • Drop-cap full bio, readable line length
  • Name moves into a sticky bar as you scroll
Design 02

Dark Header + Tabs

A brand-forward identity band with stat chips. Content is split into Overview / Full Bio / Background tabs, so you never face one wall of text.

  • Shortest scroll of the five
  • Overview tab works as a teaser
  • Good when bios vary a lot in length
Design 03

Side Drawer

A full-height panel slides in from the right, keeping the faculty grid visible. It reads like an article: a narrow column, section jump links and a compact sticky header.

  • Best fit for very long bios
  • Feels app-like, especially on mobile
  • Jump links for Background / Full Bio / Video
Design 04

Magazine Feature

A nearly full-screen editorial profile: large headline name with the background as the intro, a fact rail, full-width two-column bio with a pull quote, then specialty and video side by side.

  • Most premium / "featured profile" feel
  • Fits the most text above the fold on desktop
  • Collapses to one column on mobile
Design 05

Compact Card + Expand

Opens small and scannable, like today's popup. "Read full bio" expands it in place to show a career timeline, the full bio and the video.

  • Light first impression, depth on demand
  • Background shown as a timeline
  • Sticky collapse button once expanded
